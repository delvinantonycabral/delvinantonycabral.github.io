PROJECT 01 · FLAGSHIP CONCEPT Exploration & UI Prototyping

Caregiver App

Accessible Medicine Reminders & Caregiver Support Ecosystem

Started from a deeply personal everyday observation: helping elderly family members and grandparents reliably remember their daily medicines without feeling overwhelmed by complicated smartphone interfaces.

The concept expanded into a calm, high-legibility support experience connecting elderly users with family caregivers through QR-based pairing, clear medication schedules, and dignified, accessible mobile design.
