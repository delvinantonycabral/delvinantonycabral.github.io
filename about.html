Some days that means writing code, learning how software works or experimenting with electronics. Other days it means slowing down with a pencil sketch. Both sides matter to me: one asks for structure, the other asks me to see differently.

I'm still learning, and I prefer being honest about that. The projects here include prototypes, experiments and work in progress — each one is part of the process, not a claim to have everything figured out.
