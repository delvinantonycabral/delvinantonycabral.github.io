Between Code, Circuits & Graphite

01 · WHO I AM

A Student Who Prefers Creating Over Just Talking

Sometimes exploration means designing a digital experience. Sometimes it means experimenting with hardware on a desk. Sometimes it means sketching an idea in a notebook before writing a single line of code.

I am still learning and building my foundations, and I value honesty about where I am in that journey. Rather than claiming mastery, I focus on curiosity, consistent experimentation, and turning concepts into tangible prototypes that teach me something new every time.

02 · WHAT INTERESTS ME

Exploring Difficult Technical Ideas & Human Needs

I am drawn to problems at two ends of the spectrum: deep technical systems—like how operating-system kernels boot inside an emulator or how sensors translate physical distance into data—and deeply human challenges, like designing an interface that an elderly grandparent can use without stress.

Alongside software and hardware, I have an entrepreneurial mindset: I like looking at everyday friction and asking how a well-crafted product or tool could make life simpler and better.

03 · HOW I APPROACH PROBLEMS

Question, Deconstruct, Prototype, Refine

01. Understand the Core

Break a complex idea down to its fundamental mechanics before choosing a tool or framework.

02. Visualize Early

Sketch interfaces, system flows, and interactions on paper to spot unnecessary complexity immediately.

03. Build Working Prototypes

Test assumptions through real code, UI mockups, or microcontroller circuits to learn from practical constraints.

04 · MY CREATIVE SIDE

Pencil Sketching & The Graphite Corner

Technology is only half of how I see the world. Pencil sketching is an essential part of my creative identity. Working with graphite teaches observation, patience, structure, and subtle tonal balance—skills that directly shape how I think about typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy on the web.

To share that side of my work, I created The Graphit Corner, a dedicated digital gallery for my pencil drawings and visual studies.

05 · CURRENTLY EXPLORING & LEARNING

Where My Focus Is Right Now

  • Cross-platform & desktop application architecture using Flutter and Dart—ranging from accessible mobile caregiving concepts to the KernelLab desktop testing workbench.
  • Python programming and artificial intelligence concepts, exploring how natural-language interfaces can assist everyday desktop workflows.
  • Computer systems and cybersecurity foundations, studying how software interacts with hardware and operating environments.
  • Editorial web design, refining how ideas and creative work are presented on the internet.
